Poster Requirements

Design every carousel poster at 1920 x 1080 px.

Use a 16:9 landscape canvas. Keep important text, faces, and product visuals inside the center safe area so the hero crop works on mobile and desktop.

Use the local admin to save poster image changes. The live Vercel admin is read-only and cannot write files back to GitHub.
